Terrortops is a heavyweight robot built by Team Terrortops, which made its debut as an alternate for BattleBots World Championship VII and competed in BattleBots: Champions II. Since then, Terrortops has fought in two FaceOffs groups as well as the 2026 Pro League.

It is a four-wheel drive robot which makes use of twin articulating arms to both lift and pin other robots. These arms are coupled with a vertical spinner which could originally spin at 150mph, but was upgraded to 250mph. The combination of weaponry is designed to lift opponents and attack the vulnerable underbelly of its opposition with their disk, the largest of which was 16" diameter and made from 1" thick AR500 steel. The frame of Terrortops was milled from 6061 aluminum.

Terrortops sports a single-toothed disk as well as a variety of lifting arms for different opponents. The robot is driven by Sophia Musk-Burton, with Ben Burton operating its weaponry. Earlier renders of Terrortops had less distinct and vibrant theming, and as such, the robot was originally rejected for the season. However, upon adopting a yellow color scheme, a decorative tail at the rear of the robot and rib cage armor similar to that of Witch Doctor, Terrortops was officially accepted as an alternate.

As its name suggests, the robot has a triceratops theme, reflected in the vertical front armor and horns at the front of the robot, as well as its color scheme and markings. The name itself is a portmanteau of the words "terror" and "triceratops". Work began on building Terrortops in mid-2022[2] and the team confirmed their acceptance via Instagram on September 26, 2022.[3] Terrortops opened its BattleBots career in impressive fashion, with consecutive wins over Slammo! and Dragon King, but did not advance to the Top 32.

The rookie bot returned for BattleBots: Champions II and fought in the first Sin City Slugfest episode. However, its unbeaten streak was ended by ROTATOR in Round 1.

In April 2023, Team Terrortops officially confirmed their application had been submitted for World Championship VIII.[4] Terrortops now sported a sleeker appearance as well as long, static forks either side of the thinner, hinged forks seen during World Championship VII. Terrortops also has vulcanized rubber wheels, similar to the Brazil wheels seen on various other competitors, as well as brand new lifter configurations for different opponents. However, the season was not greenlit for television that year.

Terrortops instead fought at BattleBots FaceOffs in October 2024, as confirmed by captain Ben Burton. There, it took on Bloodsport, Switchback and Fireball in a round robin style group.[5] Terrortops won its group, marking a first piece of silverware for the team.

In 2025, Terrortops was confirmed to take part in a special FaceOffs group titled 'Battle of the Verts'. It faced off against JackPot, Orbitron and Magnitude. Ahead of these fights, the team had new disk spinners made - one for each opponent.

Terrortops returned for the 2026 BattleBots Pro League alongside 23 other competitors, partaking in the first of six four-way groups. The team boasted several new weapon options, including an asymmetrical option with a much larger single tooth, as well as a serrated blade similar to those seen on Cobalt and Big Dill in recent televised seasons. The primary weapon could now also spin at the maximum allowed 250mph in 3-4 seconds rather than taking 5-7 seconds to hit a lower speed.

Outside BattleBots[]

The team also applied for the heavyweight division of the 2023 edition of RoboGames with Terrortops, entering under the same name used for BattleBots.[6]

Terrortops destroyed its first opponent, Lay-Z-Bot in the opening fight of Day 2's heavyweight battles. It then drew White Rabbit in the second round, losing by 21-12 judges' decision, before redeeming itself with a win over King Crab in under a minute. Late in the competition, Terrortops took on a powerful drum spinner in Manta. However, both robots were involved in an arena breach during the opening thirty seconds of the fight, where one of Terrortops' forks damaged the Lexan roof and landed outside the arena, mere feet away from the camera crew.

Team Terrortops were offered the chance to continue the fight, but stated they did not feel it would be safe to do so with their remaining forks still equipped. They instead forfeited the fight, ending an impressive run in unfortunate circumstances.[4]

With driving duties shared between Sophia Musk-Burton and captain Ben Burton, Terrortops registered for RoboGames 2024 and first faced Black Dragon after receiving a Round 1 bye. Terrortops lost this fight by knockout after suffering damage to its lifter and becoming stuck on its back. It then picked up wins over Bullion and Swamp Serpent, the latter by forfeit, before achieving a ring-out victory against Mandamus. Terrortops continued on in the loser's bracket, knocking out Original Sin and then taking on BlackJack. However, Terrortops became the victim of another breach as one of the Lexan panels was struck following an impact, causing it to bow "approximately two inches" out of place, leaving a gap large enough that "you could put your hand in it", according to attendees.[7] The area had to be evacuated as a smoking Terrortops was hauled through the pit area.

Robot History[]

World Championship VII[]

Terrortops vs. Slammo!

Terrortops debuted in Episode 8 of World Championship VII, where it faced a fellow alternate in Slammo!.

Both robots immediately met in the center of the BattleBox, and Terrortops was able to get its lifting forks underneath Craig Danby's bot, tipping it backward. In the same motion, Terrortops engaged its vertical spinner and attacked the underside of Slammo!, flipping it over. Slammo! recovered, but its lifting mechanism was weighing the back of the robot down, and lifting its front wedge off the BattleBox floor. This allowed Terrortops the opportunity to lift from the front, and it capitalized, lifting again before grazing Slammo!'s armor with its spinning disk.
"I like this technique from Terrortops! Very nice."
— Kenny Florian heaps praise on Ben Burton's team
With its arms seemingly stuck in position, Slammo! adopted a sit and spin approach momentarily, but Terrortops was not deterred and drove directly into its opponent. It followed this up by getting underneath the front of Slammo! again and driving it into the rails with its lifter up. Slammo! was now stuck on its rear end, and a count began shortly after. Terrortops took the win by knockout, confirming a debut win for the rookie team.

Post-fight, Ben Burton revealed that it took several attempts to get Terrortops ready for their debut battle. Upon being loaded in the first time, one side of drive died after it became caught on the floor. Once this was fixed, the team then discovered three of their speed controllers had died from static.[8]

Terrortops vs. Dragon King

In its second and final Fight Night battle, Terrortops took on Dragon King, who previously defeated Doomba. For this battle, Terrortops was fitted with the smaller asymmetrical disk seen in its official photos.

Both robots fled their starting squares and Terrortops spun up its weapon before charging at its opponent. Dragon King then got to its flank, lowering its left-side saw but failing to grip Ben Burton's bot. Terrortops raised its lifter, threatening to deliver a blow with its vertical spinner, but instead opted to get underneath the right tread of Dragon King and lift. It then dropped Dragon King, who again failed to capitalize on getting Terrortops' frame inside its jaws.

The rookie bots tussled in the corner of the BattleBox and as Terrortops raised its lifter, the vertical spinner struck left drive pod of Dragon King, slicing into its tread and disabling that side of drive. Terrortops did not come out of this exchange unharmed, however, as a piece of Dragon King's tread was consumed by the vertical spinner, becoming lodged in a bearing and jamming the primary weapon up for good.

Despite this setback, Terrortops was still able to drive and had full functionality over its lifting mechanism. It slowly approached, sliding the forks underneath the mouth of Dragon King and flipping it over. Terrortops narrowly avoided a pulverizer shot as Will Prater's bot attempted to escape. The pneumatic ram powering its jaws then came loose, hanging limply while Terrortops got underneath again, hoisting Dragon King and performing a suplex move.
Kenny Florian: "No, and Dragon King... oh, look at that lift. Nice suplex there by Terrortops! Beautiful stuff!"
Chris Rose: "Love that! Excellent move by Terrortops."
— Chris and Kenny react as Terrortops throws Dragon King behind itself
With half the fight gone, Terrortops remained the aggressor, jamming its forks into the treads of Dragon King and using this as leverage to throw its opponent. One of its outermost forks was now bent upward, though this did not impact its ability to consistently get the better of Dragon King. Terrortops driver Sophia Musk-Burton rammed their bot into Dragon King again, looking to take their opponent into the screws and then pinning it in place as Dragon King appeared immobile in the final ten seconds of the fight.

The fight went the distance and the judges ruled unanimously in favor of Terrortops, handing the rookie team their second BattleBots win.

Terrortops vs. Mammoth (Exhibition)

Terrortops fought once more, in an exhibition battle against Mammoth. This fight was released early to the BattleBots YouTube channel ahead of the official premiere of Vengeance in Vegas II.[9]

The fight began with Mammoth immediately box-rushing Terrortops, getting its trunk caught in the rookie's lifter. It then swatted Terrortops away before trying to close down the angles and smother its opponent. Mammoth then attempted to lift Terrortops again but ended up launching itself backwards. Terrortops tried to attack the exposed wheels of Mammoth, forgoing its lifter in favor of leading with its vertical spinner. This proved effective as an attempt from Mammoth to attack led to Ricky Willems' robot damaging its own trunk.

Immediately following this, Mammoth then got an advantage and shoved Terrortops and flung off part of its decorative rib cage. Terrortops responded by targeting the long, outer forks of Mammoth, chipping away and bending them outward. These attacks also cost Mammoth the tip of its smaller, inner right fork.

Undeterred, Mammoth attempted to shove the rookie into one of the short corners next to the Upper Deck, but failed after Terrortops outdrove the former Top 16 finisher and continued to attack its forks.

Near the 1:30 mark, Terrortops successfully tore away both of Mammoth's inner forks and caused further damage to its primary weapon, leaving Ricky Willems' bot vulnerable as the fight passed the halfway point.

Terrortops then managed to get its forks under the main supporting beam of Mammoth's chassis and lifted its considerably larger opponent.

Ben Burton's team continued to attack Mammoth, breaking the middle support pole in the process while Mammoth ripped all of the decorative on the right side of Terrortops.

Nearing the end of the fight, Terrortops still proceeded to attack the front of Mammoth until it got swatted into one of the short corners of the Upper Deck at the 0:40 mark. Both robots survived the full three minutes and spent the rest of the fight attacking each other in said short corner.

Terrortops was declared the winner by judges' decision. Post-fight, Ben Burton revealed that after the fight, Mammoth weighed "20-30lbs" less than it did beforehand.[10]

BattleBots: Champions II[]

Terrortops vs. ROTATOR

Looking to extend its unbeaten competitive record, Terrortops returned for BattleBots: Champions II and took on ROTATOR in the opening round.

Terrortops was quick to raise its lifter as ROTATOR swung around to evade the rookie robot's initial charge. Victor Soto's bot swung around to drive face-first into the front of its opponent before finding itself chased by the World Championship VII alternate. ROTATOR then swiped Terrortops and flung it over, but Ben Burton's bot righted itself.
Kenny Florian: "Nice little self-right but dealing with some drive issues. I don't think Terrortops liked that hit from ROTATOR."
Chris Rose: "No, not at all."
— Kenny Florian and Chris Rose observe Terrortops' drive issues

At this stage, Terrortops was experiencing drive issues as a result of the front wedge being bent down and lifting the wheels off the ground. This allowed ROTATOR to attack without fear. Despite these issues, Sophia Musk-Burton was able to arc the robot towards the center of the BattleBox. The former Most Destructive Award winner continued to attack the wounded Terrortops, threatening to carry it to the Upper Deck, but the rookie's vertical spinner slammed into the steel floor and let it escape.

ROTATOR used its pointed front to drive Terrortops toward the short corner, who waved its lifter in a desperate attempt to shake free. Continuing its dominance, Victor Soto's robot retreated to spin up to speed before unleashing a sideswipe on Terrortops, flipping it over again.
Kenny Florian: "Impressed with the durability of Terrortops..."
Chris Rose: "Yeah."
Kenny Florian: "This is their first year in here against a veteran bot in ROTATOR. And they may be behind on the scorecards but their weapon is still active and they are hanging on"
— Kenny Florian praises Terrortops for staying in the fight

Terrortops was taken to the short corner, where it suffered several blows from the 100lb pulverizer and saw its decorative ribcage begin to crack. ROTATOR backed away before nearing closer to assess Terrortops' mobility.
The rookie bot was no longer moving and was counted out, confirming its elimination in the opening round of the bracket.

BattleBots FaceOffs[]

Terrortops vs. Fireball

Terrortops returned to the arena in October 2024, where it fought in BattleBots FaceOffs. Equipped with its serrated blade and twin hinged fork setup, the first of its three opponents was Fireball.

Terrortops darted across the arena as Fireball evaded the box-rush, getting its weapon up to speed. Sophia Musk-Burton turned Terrortops to face the oncoming Fireball, happy to engage with the superior front ground game. This resulted in Fireball being flipped over just moments into the fight.

Ben Burton raised Terrortops' lifter as it hunted down the fleeing Fireball, and a second hit saw Brian Boxell's bot hurtling towards the corner of the BattleBox. Terrortops lowered its arm as Fireball struggled to recover, using it to lift its opponent against the arena wall before Fireball reversed away from trouble.

Fireball returned to the center of the arena and Terrortops positioned itself for another attack. Using its disk, Terrortops threw Brian Boxell's machine into the air. This attack ripped off part of Terrortops' inner-left fork, but left the dinosaur-themed machine otherwise unscathed.

By contrast, Fireball landed in the red starting square and appeared less controlled as it rolled against the spiked wall of the BattleBox. Terrortops approached and, with one final attack, threw Fireball over the wall and out of the arena.
Steve Judkins: "Ohh, he's done it!"
Bil Dwyer: "It's over! It's in the no-bot zone!"
Steve Judkins: "Terrortops has put him into the no-bot zone!"
— Bil and Steve react as the fight comes to an end
This sealed Terrortops' first win of the group by knockout.

Terrortops vs. Bloodsport

Another of Terrortops' opponents was Bloodsport.

Bloodsport quickly got its blade up to speed and swiped the front wedge of Terrortops, but found itself cornered as its opponent showed little fear, opting to start aggressive with its lifter fully back. Terrortops was able to destabilize the horizontal spinner, getting in underneath and battering against Bloodsport's frame in the opening moments of the battle.

After riding over the top of its opponent, Bloodsport's bar returned to speed in spite of a rear attack from Ben Burton and Sophia Musk-Burton's bot. Bloodsport was flung into the Upper Deck thanks to Terrortops' next hit, swiping the defensive plow of Terrortops again and following through by snapping away some of Terrortops' skeletal top armor. Bloodsport reversed into its onrushing opponent in an attack which spun Terrortops around but propelled Bloodsport across the length of the BattleBox, landing upright but not before slamming into the wall.

Both machines turned to face one another again, meeting head-on and again throwing each other to opposite ends of the arena. This attack seized Terrortops' blade and loosened its ablative armor, but also deformed the self-righting pole of Bloodsport. This came off completely as Justin Marple's machine bounced into the Upper Deck, but his team sensed an opportunity with its opponent's disk dead less than a minute into the fight.

Terrortops tried to control Bloodsport as damage began to rack up on its front plow, retracting the lifter but opting against feeding it into Bloodsport's horizontal spinner. It was able to achieve this seconds later, however, using the lifter as a clamp to pin Bloodsport against the Upper Deck as it became evident that earlier attacks had totally deformed the bulkheads for Terrortops' vertical spinner. Terrortops then fully lowered its lifter for the first time in the battle, sensing that a flip or fortuitous deflection could render Bloodsport immobile.

Approaching the halfway mark, Bloodsport had lost a wedgelet as well as its self-righter, but was ahead on damage. Bloodsport's team knew they had to continue attacking, bouncing awkwardly as the horizontal spinner was hounded by Terrortops in the following thirty seconds. With a minute remaining, Bloodsport and Terrortops met head-on once more, but this time, Bloodsport was thrown upside-down and landed inverted. While Terrortops spun to celebrate, Bloodsport spun wildly on what remained of its pole, showcasing impressive gymnastics but struggling to return the bot to its wheels.

Terrortops patiently held back to wait for the count, which commenced with under twenty seconds remaining. With that, Terrortops picked up its second win of the group, advancing to 2-0.

Post-fight, Ben Burton spoke about their tactic of purposefully keeping the weapon arm out of harm's way and remaining hopeful of a win despite losing their spinner. A separate comment from Ben Burton explained how they sought confidence in starting as the aggressor as Bloodsport's spinner sat higher than the position of their drive wheels.[11]

Terrortops vs. Switchback

Last up for Terrortops was Switchback. For this battle, Terrortops used its 'Snoot' configuration, consisting of a tall, narrow plow sitting in front of the vertical spinner. Due to damage sustained in their previous fight, Team Terrortops swapped over to their other frame.

The fight started with Terrortops quickly moving out of the red square, meeting Switchback on the opposite side of the BattleBox. After a short period where each machine vyed for an opening, the front wedge of Terrortops locked into the gap that housed Switchback's eggbeater, giving it purchase to push the latter into the arena wall. The impact threw Switchback backwards and down onto the spinner of Terrortops where another hit, this time weapon-on-weapon would occur, flipping Switchback upside-down and breaking its eggbeater spinner.

Switchback self-righted as Terrortops repositioned and went for another push on the its opponent. Terrortops then quickly turned frontways, lining up a flip on Switchback which sent them momentarily in the air. This flip, plus the previous attack, would cause the side armour of Switchback to come into contact with the eggbeater, leaving it with mobility issues.

Terrortops made contact with Switchback again, briefly coming into contact with the latter's spinner. Terrortops lifted the wedge up to give themselves better contact with their spinner, which sent Switchback into the screws, upside-down for a second time. This second attack led to Greg Needel's robot briefly emitting smoke. Switchback moved for a few seconds more before becoming immobile.

Suddenly, Switchback started to move under its own power once again, going into a series of spins. Terrortops immediately went in again for another shove on Switchback, suspending them on top of Terrortops leaving them to be pushed into the arena wall once again. Switchback, now with its weapon completely dead, stopped moving for a second and final time, and was subsequently counted out. Terrortops therefore won its third and final FaceOffs match, securing a 3-0 record and the Giant Washer.

Terrortops vs. Orbitron

Terrortops later competed in a special FaceOffs group called 'Battle of the Verts'. Its first opponent was Canadian representative Orbitron and was armed with a combination of wedgelets and forks to counter the Canadian machine.

Terrortops held back as the fight began, allowing Orbitron to approach. Orbitron showed its side to Terrortops briefly, but the former FaceOffs group winner was unable to capitalize with its lifter.
Steve Judkins: "They're circling... feeling each other out, I think, Bil."
Bil Dwyer: "Oh, it's kinda like a couple of awkward teenagers at a high school dance!"
— Steve and Bil comment on the cagey start to the battle
Orbitron sharply turned and showed its opposite flank to Terrortops, allowing it to slide its forks underneath and flip Orbitron over. Terrortops also dealt a follow-up attack which seized up the self-righting arm of Orbitron.

Terrortops pushed its opponent towards the short corner anticipating it to recover quickly, but it became apparent that Orbitron's self-righter was non-functional. As a result, Orbitron was immobile and Terrortops began to celebrate. Its win was confirmed and Terrortops' winning streak in FaceOffs extended to four battles.

Despite the disappointing display from their opponent, Terrortops' co-captains spoke highly of Orbitron, with Sophia Musk-Burton calling it "an extremely cool system".

Terrortops vs. Magnitude

Terrortops next took on Chad New and Magnitude, which had also won its first battle. For this battle, Terrortops was fitted with its steep plow, as seen in its FaceOffs win over Switchback.

The fight began with neither robot making an obvious lunge towards each other, both cautiously approaching each other with both weapons fully spun up until Magnitude made the first engagement on Terrortops, flipping the latter upside-down briefly. A quick self-right from Terrortops followed and they attempted to drive away from Magnitude to make space and reset, but Magnitude quickly responded with another hit, which sent Terrortops into the air.

A third hit sent Terrortops landing on top of the arena wall, near the killsaws, leading to it being stuck briefly. Using their lifter, Terrortops slowly moved off the top of the wall onto the killsaws, which moved them back onto the floor upside-down. Chad New's machine landed another hit on them as they attempted to reset.

During that previous flurry of hits from Magnitude's eggbeater, Terrortops lost drive on one side, resulting in more continuous hits, culminating in one more that sent Terrortops as high as the top of the pulverizer.

Terrortops was then deemed immobilized by the referees, making Magnitude the winner of the fight and putting Terrortops at 1-1 for the group so far.

Terrortops vs. JackPot

Last up for Terrortops was a winless JackPot, whose team were desperate to avoid a 0-3 record. Learning from their RoboGames defeat to Jeff Waters' team, Terrortops was fitted with additional ¼" hardened steel armor at the front. Terrortops' plow was swapped back out for its more long, hinged fork setup.

As Terrortops led with its lifter down, JackPot approached with its arms raised, leading with its spinning blade. The pair collided and Terrortops utilized its forks to tip the Vegas bot back but failing to flip it over. Both disks made contact as sparks flew, but Terrortops quickly took control of the situation, driving JackPot back and dealing glancing shots to its side and rear end.
"Terrortops relentless... no breathing room for JackPot."
— Bil Dwyer reacts to Terrortops' strong start
Terrortops drove to the center of the arena as JackPot lay dormant beside the pulverizer, as the previous assault had dislodged JackPot's gearbox. Despite Ace's best efforts to intervene, a count on JackPot followed, sealing a 2-1 record for Team Terrortops and a share of the Giant Washer.
"We managed to get a hit on their drive side, which dislodged their gearbox so that they couldn't drive anymore. And in a fight like this, when you can't drive well, you're- you've lost."
— Ben Burton post-fight

Terrortops vs. Valkyrie

Terrortops fought in the 2026 BattleBots Pro League, where its first opponent came in the form of undercutter Valkyrie. It was equipped with its large plow aiming to deflect hits from its opponent's powerful spinner.

Beginning the fight with its lifter fully back, Terrortops box-rushed Valkyrie, immediately throwing its opponent several feet into the air. Valkyrie landed inverted and suffered a second and third hit soon after as Terrortops proved the early aggressor. Leanne Cushing's machine struggled to drive away as, with its blade spinning, the robot balanced on its nose and its wheels were off the ground.
Chris Rose: "Whoa, this is way different than the last time we saw Terrortops!"
Kenny Florian: "Yeah, no question about that. What an improvement in that robot."
— Chris and Kenny react to Terrortops' early dominance compared to the last time they saw it in World Championship VII

As Terrortops poised itself for another attack, a number of weapon-on-weapon hits caused the vertical spinner of Terrortops to stop spinning freely as its weapon hub bearings had been damaged. A snapped weapon belt also lay on the arena floor between both robots. Valkyrie's weapon was still spinning, but its drive was seriously compromised from damage to its drive sprocket, combined with being upside-down.

Chris Rose: "Okay, the weapon's spinning. That's good."
Kenny Florian: "Yes. But it is upside-down."
Chris Rose: "That's bad."
Kenny Florian: "Yes."
— Chris and Kenny commentate over Valkyrie's struggles
Terrortops left Valkyrie twitching in the corner as, despite the team's encouragement, its limited mobility was deemed insufficient and a count commenced. The knockout was declared after 77 seconds, temporarily sitting Terrortops second in its group.

Terrortops vs. Manta

Terrortops also took on two-time RoboGames heavyweight champion, Manta.

Terrortops vs. Skorpios

Terrortops took on veteran competitor Skorpios during the first group of BattleBots Pro League. For this battle, Team Terrortops installed additional armor to protect the belt for their spinner and the lifter chain.[12]

Trivia[]

  • Terrortops was one of only two robots in the 2022 competition to have a female driver, the other being Valkyrie.
  • Terrortops was originally rejected for World Championship VII, but was later accepted as an alternate after adopting its dinosaur theme.[4]
  • For World Championship VII, Terrortops was given the Pit number eight.
  • According to Zach Lytle of Skorpios, Terrortops was the first ever BattleBots rookie to pass safety on their first attempt.
  • The Terrortops Logo is based on the logo for the popular book/film series Jurassic Park. In addition, the team are dressed as paleontologists to carry on the theme.
  • Terrortops' mascot is a small brown triceratops nicknamed "Chonk". Victoria Tomozei, when asked about the name, explained "because he's chonky".
  • The CAD which would gain Terrortops acceptance into World Championship VII was made in just one day.[4]
  • Terrortops was originally called Attitude Adjuster when the team first applied, but was renamed Terrortops to suit its dinosaur theme.
  • Terrortops ended World Championship VII's main season as one of only two unbeaten alternates, a feat shared by Double Tap.
